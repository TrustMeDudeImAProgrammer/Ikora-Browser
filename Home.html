Hello! Welcome to Ikora “ih-KOH-ruh” ( /ɪˈkoʊrə/ ), a open-source browser started as a small passion project.

Controls:

  • First if you look up at the top of your screen that toolbar? That is your main interaction point, your controls, this is where you search, handle tabs, reload! The far left of this toolbar is the "Back ⤶" button, which is to go back to the previous page, the next button is "Forward ⤷" if you misclick "Back ⤶" and need to go forward a page! Then "Reload ⟳" to fully restart the page, finally "Home ⌂" to take you back to this page!
  • After that is the tab bar, a little different from modern browsers, but you still swap tabs by tapping the corresponding tab and create them by the + button, every new tab will be this page!
  • Then the the settings button "🌣" to well open the settings! Right know it's only one the default search engine, this will probably undergo change!
  • Next, the little "➜]" button the last on the right of the toolbar, it to close the window and end the process, useful to not crash python!
  • Finally well where you're reading this! The main visible content of the pages.

Hope this helps!
